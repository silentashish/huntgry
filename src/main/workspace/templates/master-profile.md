# Master Profile

The single source of truth. Every tailored resume is a *selection* from this
file, never an addition to it. Keep it exhaustive and unpolished: include work
that will not fit on any resume, so there is material to choose from when a
posting asks for something unexpected.

Two conventions the resume-tailor renderer understands and preserves:

- `**double asterisks**` around the impact phrase of a bullet become real bold
  in the PDF. Bold the metric or headline result, nothing else.
- Links ride along as URLs next to their display text, e.g.
  `github.com/yourhandle (link: https://github.com/yourhandle)`. A role can carry
  a `Project link:` line; projects, certifications and publications carry their
  verification links.
